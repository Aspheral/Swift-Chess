## About

**Swift** is an experimental chess engine from **Aspheral**. Its goal is deliberately different from the usual engine arms race: rather than trying to imitate a perfect tactical machine, Swift is being designed to play like a strong human around **1650 Elo**.

That means the interesting question is not simply *"What is the objectively best move?"* It is *"What move would a clever, practical human actually find and play?"*

Swift-Chess is the home of that experiment.

## Philosophy

<div align="center">

| Principle | Direction |
|:---:|---|
| **Humanity** | Prefer understandable, practical decisions over sterile perfection. |
| **Strength** | Play intelligently while retaining realistic human limitations. |
| **Character** | Allow the engine to have a recognizable playing style. |
| **Simplicity** | Keep the design purposeful instead of piling on complexity for its own sake. |

</div>

## Status

> 🚧 **Early development**
>
> Swift is actively being developed. Architecture, evaluation, search, and playing strength may change substantially as the project evolves.

The current repository should be treated as an experimental engine rather than a finished chess product.

## Development

Swift is developed as a personal chess-engine project by **Aspheral**.

The repository will evolve alongside the engine, with experiments and improvements informing future versions rather than locking the project into a rigid design too early.
