# Swift Chess: road to 2100, November 14, 2026

## Goal and rating discipline

The goal is a **human-like 2100 Chess.com Rapid player**. The most recent user-supplied Chess.com game (November 9, 2026, SwiftChessTV vs AfLingga) lists Swift at **2027 Rapid**. That is a reported *account* rating from a historical game, not a measured rating of this new source revision. An earlier 200-game Stockfish ladder produced 1632 benchmark-calibrated Elo for an older build (95% interval 1580–1684). These numbers measure different versions and populations and cannot be conflated.

We do not treat uncompleted games as draws, claim a Stockfish-equivalent rating from a few games, or describe green CI as evidence of improved chess strength.

## Experiment A: king-ring static pressure, rejected

[PR #52](https://github.com/Aspheral/Swift-Chess/pull/52) explored a bounded penalty for several enemy pieces attacking squares near Swift's king before an actual check. The exact AfLingga game showed the intended signal: 0 in the earlier sample and 53 centipawns in the position after 26...Rh2.

Equal-depth A/B tests against frozen main selected the **same move** on the starting position, Kiwipete, and two AfLingga checkpoints. An eight-game color-reversed depth-3 mini-match yielded 0 wins, 0 losses, 1 draw, and **7 unresolved positions at the 64-ply cap**. The original implementation was slower and the optimized one did not establish an advantage. The experiment was closed without merging.

## Experiment B: credible major-piece capture search order

[PR #53](https://github.com/Aspheral/Swift-Chess/pull/53) addresses a narrower concern. Before the change, all captures were prioritized over quiet moves by MVV-LVA, including a queen capturing a defended rook and losing an exchange.

The final proposal:
- Applies a legality-aware static exchange estimate **only for queen/rook captures of a less valuable piece at the root of the main search** when the destination is attacked and the net exchange loses at least 150 centipawns.
- Moves such captures later in search **ordering only**. All legal moves still get searched, evaluation is unchanged, and forced sacrifices remain possible.
- **Does not demote captures when Swift is in check.** The AfLingga game proves why: 28.Qxh2 is the best immediate defense, even though the queen is lost for a rook; 28.Kd1 instead permits 28...Qd2#.
- Uses the true search ply for quiescence move ordering rather than treating every quiescence node as root ply zero. This preserves killer-move context.

The first unrestricted SEE version reduced node counts in some positions but was noticeably slower in the browser-relevant budget. It was rejected. The optimized **root-only** version completed a clean comparison and kept the known tactical answers.

[Root-only A/B run #43593568950](https://github.com/Aspheral/Swift-Chess/actions/runs/43593568950) compared the candidate against a frozen November 9 main engine. All four fixed-depth positions had unchanged selected moves; node/time costs were close to baseline in the balanced opening and Kiwipete positions, with some noisy variations in the two AfLingga samples.

This is a regression smoke test, **not** an Elo improvement. No new Chess.com, Stockfish, or human-like performance score is claimed. The exact historical tests and the complete automated suite are more important than an artificial victory count.

The temporary frozen engine and long A/B tests were removed from the final release branch to keep routine CI responsive. Their original code and game records remain recoverable from the branch's history and linked workflow logs. The smaller chess-correctness regression tests remain in the release.

## Next serious strength measurement

For a credible 2100 claim, keep one candidate and baseline pinned to exact commits, play many paired openings in both colors at a controlled clock, record **completed** games, compare win/draw/loss and confidence intervals, and check timeouts and tactical loss rates. Independently review a fresh sample of Chess.com rapid games. Continue reporting plan consistency and human-style thinking separately from chess strength.

**Status: 2100 is not verified.** The live site should display the latest fully tested main build, not a failed strength experiment.
